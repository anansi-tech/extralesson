05 · Refusals
Seven refusals, one pattern
Every panel is the same four parts in the same order: the boundary named in a label · one sentence saying what is not available and why · what remains true, so the student knows nothing is lost · one action. The paywall is the only one whose action is red — the rest are outline, because they are not sales.
The pattern
1 · label, ink or amber
2 · one sentence, 15px serif
3 · what remains true, dim 13px
4 · one action, 44px
5 · one quiet link, optional
A refusal never uses the red cross, and never says “sorry”. The cross is for a wrong answer; a boundary is not a mistake the student made.
