  2. (b) [2] Calculate the area of triangle ABC. i: 47.6

The working for (b) — take 1
  • 8 × 11.9 = 95.2

The case is the one admin screen with a red primary, and it is “Reply by email” — the only thing on the page that reaches the student. “Mark as reviewed” is the mono-caps secondary: it acts on the record, not on the person.

The photograph is the evidence, so it sits with the read rather than in a rail — you compare the lines against the page, and at 1280 they stay in one column together while the criterion and decision move right.

The decision keeps its red bar because it is quoting a withheld mark, which is the one place red is correct here. An awarded row would carry the green bar instead.
