Worked solution

(a) tan 34° = AB / BC, so BC = 8 / tan 34° = 11.9 cm (1 d.p.).

(b) Area = ½ × BC × AB = ½ × 11.9 × 8 = 47.6 cm².

(c) AC = 8 / sin 34° = 14.3 cm, so the perimeter is 8 + 11.9 + 14.3 = 34.2 cm, which is less than 35 cm.
