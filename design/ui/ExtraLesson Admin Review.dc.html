FINAL ANSWER: $2,712 · $312 · 13%
Worked solution

Deposit = 20% × $2,400 = $480.

Instalments = 18 × $124 = $2,232, so the hire purchase price is $480 + $2,232 = $2,712.

The difference is $2,712 − $2,400 = $312, and $312 ÷ $2,400 × 100 = 13%.

Misconceptions
  • Deposit left out of the total ($2,232)
    The instalments are what is left after the deposit, not the whole price.
  • Percentage taken of the wrong base (11.5%)
    The extra is compared with the cash price, which is what it would have cost instead.

Approve stays the one red action. Edit and Reject are outline and equal to each other — the same relationship the student screens use for revisit and diagnostic. Reject carries red text, not a red fill: it is a decision, not the page’s purpose.

A flag is a reason to look, never a verdict. Red-barred for warn, paper-deep on note ground for the quieter kind — and neither one blocks approval.

“The only evidence” is the one chip that changes a decision: it means rejecting this question leaves an objective with nothing, so editing is usually the better move. That is why it is red and sits above the meta line.
